## New findings
**Program**
- **N1 [S2] `/state` `story` is truncated by compaction.** After 101 rows `story` has 18 entries starting at "Ich frage den Mönch nach dem Weg … (Tag 2)" (V). A page reload after turn ~35 therefore loses the history; any poller diffing by length sees "no narration". Fix: keep a separate display log (not `msgs`) for the UI; or persist story lines in the session.
- **N2 [S2] Kill detection requires the target's name in the cue sentence.** `markKillIntent` (World.swift:443) needs the NPC name in the same or previous sentence as a lethal cue; narrations written with pronouns ("Der Dolch sitzt tief in seiner Brust … Blut") miss it. Plus no oracle on kill actions (tangential question instead). Fix: allow the single present NPC addressed by the player verb as implicit subject; make the harness ask "Ist der Treffer tödlich?" for kill verbs.
- **N3 [S3] `[me]` line replaced by an invented sentence on ContentRule hits** (`Ich will etwas Unüberlegtes tun, aber ein Geräusch lenkt mich … (Kinder bleiben unverletzt und unbedroht.)`): the story log shows this instead of the typed input, and the deflection scene continues in the same invented direction for the next turns (drums, tooth). Consider showing the original text and keeping the info line only.
- **N4 [S3] P4 gate too leaky both ways** (7/10 risky, 4/10 trivial); trivial fires produce meaningless questions. Needs verb-class lists on the harness side instead of model choice.
- **N5 [S3] Loophole:** thread with no clue entries can be `/geloest`ed immediately.

**Model logic**
- **M1 [S2] Kill actions ignored by narration** (Oruiel case): action not narrated, no consequence.
- **M2 [S3] German garble persists:** "pornoisiert vom Rauch" (torture scene, after a retry), "Husarenschuhe"-style compounds, "Das Schrei".
- **M3 [S3] Oracle questions remain tangential/unrelated** to the action (see P4 list) and NEIN/JA results are then applied to the wrong thing.
- **M4 [S3] Thread titles from gen are literal term echoes** ("Wie beginnt Pest in Bergdorf jetzt").
