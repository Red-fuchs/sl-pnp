## Verified (OK)
- Costing per spec: major 4 / minor 2, flaws give back; budget left = 20 - pos + neg (Mara 14, Torvin 6, Ilse 24 in the final run). Leftover default = Taler (x10).
- Budget enforced: `SR.fit` drops positives that overdraw (unit-tested); `/budget` over the leftover is refused ("nur 6 Punkte übrig"), state unchanged.
- Leftover conversion: Taler 1 pt = 10, item 2, +1 SP max 3, unspent points become Taler (Mara 70 Taler + Seil, Laterne + SPmax 6; Ilse 120 Taler + 3 items + SPmax 7).
- Pillars always 30/40/50 (distinct); Foundry sheet shows pillars, SP x/y, Wunden, Taler, traits (items with ±), gear after sync.
- Foundry check: target = pillar + trait + difficulty + SP; DoS/DoF correct (e.g. 34 vs 90 = 6 DoS); SP on the card (pre-roll and button) = SP deducted in Foundry AND sl (checked 12 times, equal).
- sl `/roll`: target = pillar + fitting trait (word match, also partial: "Behinderte Hand" matched "Taube, krumme rechte Hand" = -20) + difficulty + penalties; degrees correct, 01-05/96-100 rules by unit tests.
- No leaks: no `[Fakt]`, `[Orakel]`, `Regie:`, `Du:`, `Figuren:`, `Spielfigur:`, system-prompt text in any chat message (3 runs, 0 hits).
