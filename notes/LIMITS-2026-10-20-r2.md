## Hard limits (unchanged or new)
1. Web input truncated silently at 2000 chars; body > 64 KB -> 413 (V).
2. Second `/say` while busy is dropped, no queue (V).
3. Compaction at 14000 cached tokens (~turn 33-37); recap = last 12 scene summaries.
4. 3 attempts per generation stage, then last output accepted (genCall); thread stage `maxTok 380` is hit on llg (N1).
5. Säulen values clamp -1..+2, at most 3 talents.
6. 6 oracle questions per scene; S (tension) floor 1.

## New findings, ranked
Severity: S1 breaks a feature, S2 clear failure, S3 polish. P = program, M = model logic.

### S1

**N1. [P] llg default: thread generation fails in 6 of 6 worlds -> no threads, no clues, no Spur targets; world gen 3x slower**
- Repro: `./play.sh web --port N --debug`, `/welt "Bergdorf im Alpental, die Pest geht um, ein Wanderarzt"`, `/welt start`. Result: info `Faden 1 fehlgeschlagen – weiter`, `Faden 2 fehlgeschlagen – weiter`, `Welt spielbar (92 s)`, `world.json straenge: []`, `hidden.json clues: []` (V: llgt, sec, sec-2, saeul, death, chaos-orakel; gen.secs `Faden 1 übersprungen 28-36 s`). Same text with `--grammar xg`: two threads in 6-12 s, world ready in 23-36 s (V, 6/6 worlds).
- Evidence: limit2-llgt/world.json `secs: {'Faden 1 übersprungen': 36, 'Faden 2 übersprungen': 36 …}`; limit2-xgt/world.json secs `Fäden 1/2: 7.7`.
- Cause (suspected): `genCall` retries 3x on `TruncatedError` (maxTok 380, WorldGen.swift:306/221); the llg grammar for `GenGr.thread` seems not to bound field length (xg `clip`/limits are not converted into the Lark grammar), so the output always overruns and all 3 attempts are wasted (3 x 12 s).
- Impact: a player on the default engine gets worlds without any investigation thread; `/welt debug` shows `Hinweise:` empty, Spur has nothing to track. This also invalidates tier/clue tests for default play.
- Fix: carry length limits into the Lark converter for the thread grammar (or raise maxTok / fall back to xg for gen stages); log the truncation reason; add a regression test "thread gen succeeds on llg".

**N2. [P] Krypta/clue places are unreachable: place-name mismatch between hidden.json and world visits**
- Evidence (analyst, xgt): clue `at: "Die Krypta der Grenzsteine"` but the visit writes `Krypta der Grenzsteine`; both exist in world.json orte; Krypta visited at t5 and t32, no `enthüllt` event, clues C1/C4 never found; the Krypta questions got NEIN. Same pattern in round 1 (normal: "Die Schmugglergilde").
- Cause: place keys are not normalised (article, case) when matching clue `at`/NPC `ref` against the player's location.
- Fix: normalise (strip leading article, lower-case, de-umlaut) in one `placeKey()` used by gen, move and reveal; merge duplicates on creation.

**N3. [P/M] Meta-narration fallback replaces the player's turn with a stock sentence**
- Evidence: saeul T27 `/frage Ist die Brücke morgen noch intakt?` -> NEIN+, narration only "Die Szene verharrt für einen Augenblick." after 2x "Erzählung verworfen (Meta-Text oder fremde Zeichen)", retry prefill 10067 tok, 35 s turn. dark T10 (derbe Beschimpfung) same sentence after 2 retracts, dark turn max 37 s.
- Cause: V6 "meta narration on the last attempt is replaced" yields a constant fallback; rejection triggers are unexplained (xgt rows 16 and 29 were legit questions).
- Fix: when the last attempt is rejected, fall back to a short in-world line built from the oracle answer + last action, log the rejected text (debug), and lower the false-positive rate of the meta filter; avoid full re-prefill on retry (cache rollback).

**N4. [P] Säulen: no odds shift on `/frage`; rules do almost nothing visible**
- Repro: `/regeln saeulen`, `/saeulen kampf=2 wissen=-1`, `/talent Zweihandschwung`, then `/frage Kann ich den Wachposten mit einem Hieb niederschlagen?` (Kampf+2) vs `/frage Kann ich die alte Schriftrolle entziffern?` (Wissen-1), same tension S=5.
- Evidence (V, saeul sse.log cards): all 8 `/frage` cards have `p=50` for all four arts (Kampf, Wissen, Sozial, sonst); with `/regeln frei` also `p=50` (4 cards) -> frei unchanged (good) but saeulen identical. Free-text actions (2 combat, 2 knowledge, 2 social, rows 12-17) fired **no** oracle at all in saeulen mode, so no shift and no dice. One card showed `p=86` with label "offen" (Gibt es Wachen am Tor?) = label/p mismatch.
- Cause: art classification (`gate.art`) only runs on GM-initiated oracle calls; the `/frage` path (`Runner.frage`) bypasses `oddsShift`.
- Fix: apply `regel.oddsShift` in `Runner.frage` (classify art with the same cheap classifier) and show the effective odds word in the tool line; add free-text risk gate when `regeln == saeulen`.

**N5. [P] Death handling is incomplete**
- Evidence (death player, V partly): Yorric shot -> `/welt` shows `tot` only after the following turn (kill turn T3: not yet; T4: `Yorric (Kräuterfrau · tot)`); Thoaule stabbed with dagger, narrated "sinkt sterbend": world logs "Leben -1" only, stays `hier · misstrauisch`; innkeeper Brannoc stabbed -> relabelled `gegangen`; rules: Oruor dead at T4 (oracle JA+), at T22 both Oruor and Amshon are back as `hier`, no dead flag. Reported/claimed deaths ("Reisender erzählt, Thoaule sei tot", "Ich weiß, dass Thoaule tot ist"): ignored, no contradiction handling (acceptable). Narrative resurrection of a dead NPC: ignored by the world (stays tot) but GM moved the scene (fine).
- `/lebt`: works for dead NPC (`Yorric lebt`), clear messages for living NPC / unknown / empty (`keine tote Figur '…'`), no crash (V `/lebt`, `/lebt Niemand`).
- Fix: mark `tot` in the same turn as the delta (not next), add a `tot` field to the delta schema triggered by "sinkt sterbend/stirbt/tot" cues plus oracle JA on "ist X tot?", remove dead NPCs from `hier` on scene sync.
